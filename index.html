02 / HOW WE LEARN

Our previous studio portrait workshop with Gareth took photographers from the tools of the trade to classic Hollywood light, experimental mixed-light setups, and a final conversation about the images. That's the kind of hands-on learning we believe in.

01 / DISCOVER

Get to know the tools.

Lights, modifiers, stands and grip equipment. Understand what they do and what they make possible.

02 / BUILD

Shape the light.

Learn by constructing a photographic setup, not simply watching someone else do it.

03 / MAKE

Take the photograph.

Work with the equipment, shoot, experiment, make mistakes and try again.

04 / REFLECT

Talk about the work.

Share pictures, compare choices and leave with ideas worth taking into the next shoot.
