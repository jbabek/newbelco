      <p class="eyebrow">Recruitment advertisement · Draft 1</p>
      <h2 style="font-size:clamp(35px,4vw,51px)">GP Veterinarian — Internal Medicine Focus</h2>
      <p style="font-weight:700;color:#53657a">Veterinary Specialty and Imaging (VSI) · Hong Kong</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top:34px">An opportunity to practice veterinary medicine a little differently.</h3>
      <p>At Veterinary Specialty and Imaging (VSI), we believe that exceptional veterinary care comes from more than medical knowledge, equipment, or qualifications. It comes from the people delivering that care, the relationships they build, and the environment in which they work.</p>
      <p>We are looking for a general practice veterinarian who shares that philosophy.</p>
      <p>This is not a traditional GP position, nor is it a junior specialist role. We are looking for a capable, curious veterinarian who enjoys the intellectual challenges of internal medicine, takes pride in developing meaningful relationships with clients, and wants to practice in a collaborative, well-supported clinical environment.</p>
      <p>We are a small, independent practice with an established internal medicine referral service and advanced diagnostic imaging facilities. We are now looking to extend the approach to client care that has shaped our specialist practice into a complementary general medicine service.</p>
      <p>We are in no hurry to fill this position. Finding the right person matters considerably more to us than finding someone with a particular number of years of experience.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top:34px">The opportunity</h3>
      <p>You will have the opportunity to develop your own medical caseload, with a particular emphasis on internal medicine, diagnostic investigations, chronic disease management, and continuity of care.</p>
      <p>Your work will include independent consultations, diagnostic procedures, outpatient CT scanning, and selected follow-up cases from our internal medicine service.</p>
      <p><strong>There are no surgical responsibilities.</strong></p>
      <p>You will work alongside Dr. Lawren Durocher-Babek, a board-certified specialist in small animal internal medicine, with opportunities for clinical discussions, case rounds, and the exchange of ideas.</p>
      <p>We believe general practitioners and specialists bring different, equally important expertise to veterinary medicine. Our goal is a collegial environment where questions are encouraged, knowledge is shared freely, and professional respect goes in every direction.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top:34px">A team built around practicing medicine</h3>
      <p>One of the things that makes VSI different is the way we organize our work.</p>
      <p>Our experienced team takes responsibility for the operational, administrative, and client-service functions of the practice, allowing our veterinarians to concentrate on clinical decisions, diagnostics, treatments, procedures, and client relationships.</p>
      <p>Our clinical and management teams work closely together, with clear responsibilities and considerable autonomy in their respective areas.</p>
      <p>We believe good veterinary medicine is a team effort. We value our nurses and support staff as professionals whose knowledge, judgment, and contributions are essential to the care we provide.</p>
      <p>We are looking for a veterinarian who enjoys working this way.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top:34px">Who we hope to find</h3>
      <p>We are less concerned about how long you have been practicing than about how you approach your work.</p>
      <p>You might be an established GP looking for a more intellectually rewarding environment. You might be a talented younger veterinarian who has developed strong clinical judgment and wants to broaden their experience. You might currently be working as a locum and be interested in building a more lasting relationship with a practice and its clients.</p>
      <p>What matters to us is that you:</p>
      <ul style="padding-left:23px;line-height:1.8">
        <li>Genuinely enjoy being a general practice veterinarian.</li>
        <li>Have a strong interest in internal medicine and diagnostic problem-solving.</li>
        <li>Are comfortable making independent clinical decisions while recognizing when collaboration or referral is appropriate.</li>
        <li>Take pride in clear, thoughtful communication with clients.</li>
        <li>Value professional curiosity, shared knowledge, and constructive case discussions.</li>
        <li>Respect and trust the contributions of nurses, colleagues, and the wider practice team.</li>
        <li>Believe that excellent client care deserves the same attention as excellent medical care.</li>
        <li>Want to continue learning and developing throughout your career.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>You should be appropriately qualified and eligible to practice veterinary medicine in Hong Kong, with sufficient clinical experience and judgment to work independently.</p>
      <p>Beyond that, we're interested in the person, not simply the CV.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top:34px">Working arrangements</h3>
      <p>We envisage a position of approximately <strong>four days per week</strong>, including opportunities to work alongside Dr. Lawren and independently manage your own caseload.</p>
      <p>We are also open to an initial commitment of <strong>two or three days per week</strong>, particularly for someone who wishes to maintain existing locum commitments while developing their role with VSI.</p>
      <p>Saturday availability would be particularly valuable, given existing client demand.</p>
      <p>The position offers:</p>
      <ul style="padding-left:23px;line-height:1.8">
        <li>A competitive base salary plus commission.</li>
        <li>Flexible discussion around the initial number of working days.</li>
        <li>No surgery.</li>
        <li>Access to CT, ultrasound, endoscopy, and advanced diagnostic capabilities.</li>
        <li>An established and experienced clinical support team.</li>
        <li>Regular opportunities to collaborate and discuss cases with a board-certified internal medicine specialist.</li>
        <li>The opportunity to develop a personally rewarding, long-term clinical practice.</li>
      </ul>
      <h3 style="margin-top:34px">Our relationship with the veterinary community</h3>
      <p>VSI has been built around collaborative relationships with general practice veterinarians throughout Hong Kong.</p>
      <p>Those relationships remain fundamental to our practice.</p>
      <p>We see specialist medicine as a resource that supports general practice, not a replacement for it. Our new GP service will operate with the same respect for referring veterinarians, professional relationships, and continuity of care that guide our existing referral service.</p>
      <h3 style="margin-top:34px">Interested?</h3>
      <p>We would welcome a confidential conversation with veterinarians who feel this might be the right professional environment for them.</p>
      <p>We are particularly interested in understanding what you enjoy about veterinary medicine, what you hope to do more of in your career, and what you believe makes a genuinely good veterinary practice.</p>
      <p>Please send us your CV together with a brief introduction telling us a little about yourself and why this opportunity interests you.</p>
      <p><strong>All inquiries will be treated confidentially.</strong></p>
      <p style="margin-top:26px"><strong>Veterinary Specialty and Imaging (VSI)</strong><br/>Hong Kong</p>
